# Awesome-Jail-Management

## Top Jail Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Booking, Custody, Inmate Tracking, Housing, Visitation, Commissary, Communications & Corrections Operations*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Jail Management**. These systems support intake/booking, classification, housing, movement tracking, incident reporting, visitation, commissary, inmate communications, and related custody operations for jails and correctional facilities.



**Examples** include Guardian RFID, Tyler Jail Manager, CentralSquare Jail, Encartele, SmartJAIL Mail, VendEngine, TurnKey Corrections, GTL Offender360, Corrisoft, and Tribridge Jail (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Production-grade jail and offender management systems are almost exclusively commercial due to security, CJIS, and liability requirements. Practical open resources are limited to experimental offender-management prototypes, visitor management tools, and prison-education stacks. This section is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Guardian RFID](https://guardianrfid.com/)**  

  RFID-based inmate location, accountability, and cell-check platform providing verifiable headcounts and movement audit trails.



- **[Tyler Jail Manager / Odyssey Corrections](https://www.tylertech.com/)**  

  Enterprise corrections and jail management capabilities within the Tyler public-safety and justice suite (booking, custody, records).



- **[CentralSquare Jail Management](https://www.centralsquare.com/)**  

  Jail management software for booking, custody, housing, rosters, and related correctional workflows.



- **[Encartele](https://www.encartele.net/)**  

  Inmate communication and related facility technology solutions used in correctional settings.



- **[SmartJAIL Mail](https://www.smartjailmail.com/)**  

  Digital mail and related inmate communication services for jails and prisons.



- **[VendEngine](https://www.vendengine.com/)**  

  Commissary, trust accounting, and inmate financial technology platform for correctional facilities.



- **[TurnKey Corrections](https://www.turnkeycorrections.com/)**  

  Corrections technology provider covering various jail and prison operational systems.



- **[GTL Offender360](https://www.gtl.net/)**  

  Offender management and related communications/technology solutions from Global Tel Link (GTL).



- **[Corrisoft](https://www.corrisoft.com/)**  

  Community corrections and offender management technology, including supervision and related tools.



- **[Tribridge Jail / related justice solutions](https://www.tribridge.com/)**  

  Justice and jail management offerings historically associated with Microsoft Dynamics–based public-sector solutions.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Corrections & Offender Management (experimental)](https://github.com/worlds-biggest-software-project/379-corrections-offender-management)**  

  AI-native open-source prototype aiming to cover intake, tracking, programmes, release planning, and community supervision in an auditable platform (not production-ready for most agencies).



- **[Jail / correctional visitor management (QR VMS) projects](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source visitor registration and QR check-in/out systems designed for correctional facility visitation tracking.



- **[Open Prison Education (OPE) stack](https://github.com/operepo)**  

  Open tools and scripts supporting offline-capable education delivery and student management inside correctional settings (e.g., Canvas-oriented workflows).



- **[Inmate education and offline learning open components](https://github.com/)**  

  Projects focused on secure, offline content delivery and LMS integration for incarcerated learners.



- **[Property and trust-fund open prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Experimental tools for tracking inmate property or simple trust balances (not a substitute for certified financial systems).



- **[Incident and log open templates](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight open logging and incident-report templates that agencies can adapt for internal use.



- **[Booking form and intake open kits](https://github.com/)**  

  Form and workflow prototypes for capturing intake data (require heavy customization and security review).



- **[Public-safety data standard open libraries](https://github.com/)**  

  Libraries implementing justice and corrections data standards that can support interoperability.



- **[Secure messaging open research tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Research-oriented open components exploring controlled communication patterns (not production inmate calling systems).



- **[Facility operations open dashboards](https://github.com/)**  

  Generic open dashboard frameworks that some agencies adapt for non-sensitive operational metrics.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Exploring experimental offender-management prototypes only in research, training, or non-production contexts.

- Using open visitor-management tools as a supplement—not a replacement—for certified jail systems.

- Supporting prison education with open LMS and offline-sync stacks while keeping custody systems commercial.

- Accepting that CJIS compliance, biometric integration, RFID accountability, certified trust accounting, and 24/7 vendor support still require commercial platforms (Guardian RFID, Tyler, CentralSquare, GTL, VendEngine, etc.).

- Focusing any open-source effort on transparency of non-sensitive processes and education rather than core custody control.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Core custody, booking, and movement must remain on validated commercial JMS platforms. Open tools may support peripheral functions (education, visitor kiosks, reporting prototypes) under strict security review. Most jails and departments of correction continue to rely exclusively on commercial jail management systems for operational and legal reasons.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Jail and corrections systems handle highly sensitive personal and security data and are subject to strict legal, constitutional, and CJIS requirements. Open-source or experimental software is generally **not** suitable as a primary custody system without extensive independent validation, security certification, and legal review. This list is not legal, security, or operational advice. Misuse of custody systems can have serious human and legal consequences.
